The opening idea01 / 05

Architecture is not a monument to itself.

A building can belong to everyone.

Locsin made architecture that met the public at full scale: theaters, churches, cultural institutions, and civic spaces. His buildings were bold without being sealed off from the life around them.

Deep shade, generous overhangs, open ground planes, and the drama of heavy forms shaped an architecture responsive to heat, rain, gathering, and movement. This is tropical modernism with a distinctly Filipino address.

Why Locsin was selected

Civic space becomes national identity.

Locsin was selected for an exhibition about civic art and national identity because his public buildings made modern Filipino culture visible in shared space. They address people at the scale of the city, not only the institution.

That generosity matters. Public architecture gives a society places in which to recognize itself: not only inside the hall, but in the threshold between the institution and the street.
